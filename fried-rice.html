Special occasions

Fried
Rice

Colourful, savoury rice filled with vegetables and the bright flavours that make celebrations special.

40 mins Total time
5 Servings
Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked long-grain rice
  • Mixed carrots, peas, and sweet corn
  • 1 onion and spring onions
  • Chicken stock
  • Curry powder and white pepper
  • Oil and salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the vegetables by washing and cutting them evenly.
  2. Heat oil and saute onion until soft.
  3. Add vegetables, curry powder, and pepper.
  4. Stir in cooked rice and chicken stock.
  5. Toss until hot, colourful, and well seasoned.

Kitchen tip Use cold cooked rice so every grain stays separate while you stir-fry.
