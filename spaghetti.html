Everyday meal

Spaghetti
with sauce

A quick, satisfying pasta meal with a rich tomato sauce and simple everyday seasonings.

30 mins Total time
3 Servings
Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 4 tomatoes or tomato puree
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • Cooking oil
  • Chilli, oregano, and black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil spaghetti in salted water until tender.
  2. Saute onion and garlic in oil.
  3. Add tomatoes and seasonings, then simmer the sauce.
  4. Drain the spaghetti and toss it through the sauce.
  5. Serve warm with your favourite garnish.

Kitchen tip Save a little pasta water to loosen the sauce and help it cling to the spaghetti.
