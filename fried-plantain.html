Comfort food

Fried
Plantain

Golden, sweet plantain slices with crisp edges and a soft centre. Perfect as a side or snack.

20 mins Total time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe plantains
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional pepper sauce

Instructions

  1. Peel the plantains and slice them diagonally.
  2. Season lightly with salt.
  3. Heat oil in a wide frying pan.
  4. Fry in batches until both sides are golden brown.
  5. Drain on paper towel and serve warm.

Kitchen tip Use ripe but firm plantains so the slices caramelise without becoming too soft.
