Family favourite

Fried
Chicken

Juicy, seasoned chicken with a crisp golden coating for a satisfying family meal.

50 mins Total time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken pieces
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 eggs
  • Garlic, ginger, and onion powder
  • Black pepper and salt
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Season chicken and allow it to marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Beat eggs in one bowl and season flour in another.
  3. Dip chicken in egg, then coat thoroughly with flour.
  4. Fry in medium-hot oil until golden and cooked through.
  5. Drain well and serve with your favourite side.

Kitchen tip Keep the oil at medium heat so the coating browns while the chicken cooks inside.
