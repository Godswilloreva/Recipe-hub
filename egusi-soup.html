Traditional favourite

Egusi
Soup

A hearty, nutty soup made with ground melon seeds, leafy vegetables, and rich Nigerian seasoning.

60 minsTotal time
6Servings
MediumDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ground egusi
  • 500 g assorted meat or fish
  • 2 cups chopped spinach or ugu
  • 1 onion and fresh pepper
  • Palm oil and stock
  • Seasoning and salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Season and cook the meat or fish until tender.
  2. Heat palm oil and soften the chopped onion.
  3. Stir in the ground egusi and cook gently until fragrant.
  4. Add stock and the cooked protein, then simmer until thick.
  5. Fold in the vegetables and cook for five minutes.

Kitchen tip Add vegetables near the end so they keep their colour and fresh flavour.
