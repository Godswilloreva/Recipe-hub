Rice
& Stew

Soft, fluffy rice served with a deeply seasoned tomato stew for a comforting homemade meal.

45 mins Total time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 3 cups rice
  • 6 tomatoes and 2 peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • Vegetable oil
  • Stock, thyme, and curry powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash and cook the rice with water and a little salt.
  2. Blend tomatoes, peppers, and onion.
  3. Fry the blended mixture until reduced and fragrant.
  4. Add stock and seasonings, then simmer until rich.
  5. Serve the stew over hot rice.

Kitchen tip Let the stew reduce properly before adding stock. It concentrates the tomato flavour.
