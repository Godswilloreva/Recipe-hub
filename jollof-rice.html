Nigerian classic

Jollof
Rice

A rich, smoky tomato rice dish that brings colour, warmth, and celebration to the table.

45 mins Total time
4 Servings
Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 large tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • Vegetable oil and stock
  • Thyme, curry powder, salt, and pepper

Instructions

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and half the onion until smooth.
  2. Fry sliced onion in oil, then add the blended pepper mixture.
  3. Cook the sauce until it thickens and the oil rises to the top.
  4. Stir in rice and stock, cover, and cook on low heat until tender.
  5. Rest for five minutes, fluff gently, and serve.

Kitchen tip Cook the pepper base patiently. That is where the deep flavour and smoky character begin.
